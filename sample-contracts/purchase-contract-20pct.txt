RESIDENTIAL PURCHASE CONTRACT

Contract Date: October 21, 2026

Property: 24 Linden Ave, Brooklyn NY (demo listing)
Seller: Sam Seller
Buyer: Bea Buyer
Seller's Attorney: Alex Attorney, Esq.

1. Purchase price. The buyer agrees to buy the property for the price agreed in the listing.

2. Deposit. Within one business day of signing, the buyer shall pay a deposit equal to 20% of the
purchase price into the escrow account held by the seller's attorney.

3. Release. The deposit is paid to the seller only after the home inspection passes, the buyer's
final walkthrough passes, and both the buyer and the seller approve closing.

4. Refund. If the inspection or walkthrough fails, the attorney returns the full deposit to the buyer.

Signed: Bea Buyer          Signed: Sam Seller
